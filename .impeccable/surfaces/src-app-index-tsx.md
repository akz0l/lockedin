## Scope

Whole native app shell (Focus, Filters, Instagram tabs) plus the Android block screen. Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (Operate surface; no image generation on this machine).

## Audience and job

The founder on their own phone, starting a focus session before work or study, checking that filters are on, and being stopped mid-impulse. Use scene: phone in hand at a desk by day and in bed at night, mixed ambient light, so both appearances follow the system and are first-class.

## Direction contract

THESIS: LockedIn reads like a precise instrument from the Vercel and Uber school: one decision per screen, state always legible. It refuses the wellness-app default of pastel gradients, mascots, streak gamification and decorative progress rings.

OWN-WORLD: Monochrome ink and paper. Pure black and white grounds, a six-step neutral ramp, 1px hairline separators, grouped inset lists. Status dots in Vercel's state vocabulary: green on, amber needs attention, red strict or blocked, gray off. Geist SemiBold for display moments, the system face for body and controls. One solid full-width ink button per screen.

STORY: The user sees within a second whether protection is on, starts a session in two taps, and when tempted meets a calm, final block screen that names the rule and the time left.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Focus tab. Large title "Focus". A status row with a dot and "3 filters on". Below, the duration as giant Geist numerals ("25:00") with presets 25/50/90/Custom, the block level control (Soft, Firm, Strict) with a one-line consequence, and on Android a blocked-apps row. The primary "Lock in" button sits full width at the bottom above the tab bar. Active session: the countdown fills the width in tabular numerals, the level dot and label under it, and the end control that the level allows.

FORM: Canon of the brief-pinned references (Vercel dashboard, Uber rider app), translated to native controls and native tabs. Signature move: giant tabular countdown plus a 2px lock line that drains across the top edge of every screen while a session runs. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
